# **App Name**: Rahul's Portfolio

## Core Features:

- Intro Section: Display a brief introduction with a personal headline: I make ML things happen.
- About Me Section: Showcase a detailed bio, with the title 'About Me' and the following text: I am a Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence graduate from IIIT Delhi, with hands-on experience in machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and large language model evaluation. My work spans both research and industry. Currently, I’m contributing to xAI as an AI Tutor, where I evaluate Grok’s agentic and multimodal abilities, and ensure alignment through rigorous evaluation frameworks. I enjoy bridging theory with practical impact, whether it’s optimizing training pipelines from hours to minutes, or designing systems that bring clarity to complex models. Outside of research and development, I love finding playful edges to otherwise serious problems, which is why I like to say: I can debug neural networks faster than I can decide what to eat for dinner.
- Work Experience Section: List professional roles and accomplishments in a modular card format. Each card displays one work experience with company name and designation at the left and timeline at the right. A dropdown arrow expands the card to show details in bullet points. A linear line runs at the left of the cards.
- My Work Section: Display portfolio items in cards, with two cards per row. Each card shows the project name, details, tech stack buttons, and a GitHub link button at the bottom left.
- Technical Skills Section: Present technical proficiencies with the title horizontally centered. Skills are displayed as buttons; hovering highlights the button and shows additional text in a small box above it.
- Education Section: Outline academic background and qualifications in cards. Each card shows the institute name and degree at the left and timeline on the right, with highlights (bullet points) below. A vertical line runs at the left of these cards.
- Get In Touch Section: Include a contact section with the title 'Get In Touch' and the text: 'My inbox is always open. Whether you have a question or just want to say hi, I’ll try my best to get back to you!' Below the text is a 'Say Hello' button (linked to email), LinkedIn/GitHub links, and the email address atharvdxb14@gmail.com.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Light gray (#D3D3D3) for text to ensure readability against the dark background.
- Background color: Dark slate gray (#2F4F4F) to establish a professional, tech-focused aesthetic.
- Accent color: Teal (#008080) for interactive elements such as buttons or links.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', serif, for headlines.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, for all paragraph text and other readable elements.
- Use simple line icons in teal to represent skills and sections.
- Implement a one-page layout with clearly defined sections, ensuring sufficient spacing between elements for easy navigation.
- Incorporate subtle, smooth scrolling animations to improve user experience when navigating between sections.
